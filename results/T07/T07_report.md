## Problems and deviations
- **Swap was not below 512 MiB.** It was 2,305 MiB at start and 1,573 MiB after Arya closed apps. A reboot was offered once and declined in favour of proceeding. Swap at the start of runs was 2.7 to 4.6 GiB, and the swap file grew from 3 to 5 GiB during the session. The baseline n=6 runs paged (+852 / +302 MiB) and the baseline n=8 run paged (+991). No V5 run grew swap by more than 99 MiB.
- **The 3% criterion is not met; the difference is itemized** (section 2) and reconciles to the cycle.
  - The largest item is the transcript V5 keeps as instructed: 2,728 cycles. That includes the L2..L4 fold rounds, which the real B'-L1 design shares once.
  - The sponge state is not used after open_stacked, so a design that shares the chain would not pay it per child. That would put the saving at about 32.8k per child (derived, not measured).
- **L1 query positions** for children ≥1 are read as unchecked computed advice (`hint_windows_exponent`), which is the task's "read from a hint" option. No Rust hint stream was added. Its cost (210 cycles) is listed separately.
- **Kept small arithmetic.** The sumcheck round arithmetic (about 5 cycles a round) and the level batching arithmetic (114 cycles) are not gated, since a runtime branch costs 3 to 5 cycles. The OOD-eq terms, residual, PoW and decode are gated.
- **Point rotation (105) is kept**, because the e3 terminal weights outside open_stacked need the terminal point and are kept as specified.
- **The final check is not removed.** For children ≥1 it runs and holds vacuously, because open_stacked returns sumcheck_target = yr_at_tail = 0. It costs 3 cycles per child.
- **Attribution tooling.**
  - T05's level labeller miscounted V5's split residual loops. It advanced a level only when the closing store line was followed directly by the same loop's code.
  - It also miscounted the gate lines inside the level loop.
  - Both are fixed in attribute_v5.py, with gate lines labelled by the region they open. On the baseline dumps it still reproduces T05 exactly.
- **Different leaves.** V5's leaves are proven by the V5 binary, so its node verifies different child proofs from the baseline's node, with different query positions. This is the source of the noise rows: L0 cap/path +28 cycles, and 42.5 in all.
- **n=2 prove-time saving is small (19.5 ms).** TOTAL_COMMITTED only drops from 2^23.842 to 2^23.745 there, at the same mu. The n=6 saving includes the baseline's mu step (26 → 25), and V5 n=6 sits only about 0.003 of an exponent below mu 26.
- **The n=8 `--repeat 1` prove times did not parse** in analyze.py (single pass). They were read from the logs (2.856 / 2.166 s), are marked paging-bound and are not used.
- The baseline n=8 footprint was 4.30 GiB, below 70% of RAM (5.6 GiB).
